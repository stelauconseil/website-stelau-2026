## Final generation prompt

Create a premium editorial hero photograph/conceptual still life for a French cybersecurity consultancy article about digital identity wallets: moving from keeping copies of personal documents to verifying only the necessary proof. Wide landscape 2:1 composition, as high resolution as possible, at least 2560 pixels wide if supported. Art direction: elegant modern financial/technology magazine, sophisticated physically realistic studio photography, bright editorial daylight, meticulously sharp tactile details, convincing glass and paper. On a pale warm grey architectural desktop, a substantial stack of blank semi-translucent identity document sleeves and dossier papers on the left progressively thins into one minimal luminous attestation card floating just above a contemporary unbranded smartphone on the right. The smartphone is laid at a gentle angle, with a refined cobalt-blue and white digital wallet interface showing one small emerald verification mark and restrained abstract lines, absolutely no readable text or invented letters. A few very subtle amber accents on the document edges, Stelau cobalt blue #0069b4, graphite grey, ivory and a restrained muted teal, no saturated cyberpunk neon. Strong clear focal point, quiet sculptural composition, soft natural shadows, beautiful light, no busy infographic, no connecting arrows, no grid of panels, no timeline. Object grouping contained within the central 80% so the image crops gracefully on mobile and blog thumbnails. No people, no real personal information, no passports with recognizable national insignia, no padlocks, no hacker imagery, no logos, no watermarks, no typography. This is a new conceptual image, not an edit or a diagram. Deliver one exquisite polished editorial image suitable for a professional consultancy website.
