## Authorising access to biometric data

For **Eviden and France’s Agence Nationale des Titres Sécurisés (ANTS)**, Stelau developed **idFod**, a signing solution for exchanges with electronic passports, electronic national identity cards and residence permits.

The document sends a challenge. Signing that challenge contributes to terminal authentication: the terminal must prove it is authorised to access sensitive data. This project addresses access to **Data Group 3 (DG3)**, which contains fingerprint images.

## CVC expertise across the whole chain

**Card Verifiable Certificates (CVC)** are central to this project. Our engineers work on encoding and decoding, **EC and RSA** public keys, creating and authenticating certificate requests, signature verification and interpreting access rights.

This expertise connects the **CVCA → DV → IS** chain to the project’s practical requirements: precise formats, cryptographic parameters, interoperability and certificate lifecycle management.

### Two SOAP functions, an operational context

The **SOAP webservices** provide the certificate chain and sign the challenge on request. Integration accounts for document delivery equipment in town halls, prefectures and consulates, as well as airport inspection systems.

The solution connects to **PKIs issuing CVCs** and to **HSMs** used to generate asymmetric key pairs and perform signatures. Protected communications complete the architecture.

## Making operations understandable

The **React** interface lets operators manage certificates, keys and users, inspect logs and monitor service status. The screenshots show CVCA, DV and IS certificate families, signing activity and renewal monitoring.

Statuses and diagnostics need to be clear. Operators must be able to identify and understand a rejected renewal request so that they can continue their checks.

## From specifications to an integrated solution

Challenge management services, PKI and HSM connections and the administration interface form a solution integrated into the ANTS operational environment. The work combines **interoperability**, **certificate management** and **control over cryptographic operations**.

The security mechanisms draw on travel document specifications: [ICAO Doc 9303, Part 11](https://www.icao.int/sites/default/files/publications/DocSeries/9303_p11_cons_en.pdf), and [BSI TR-03110](https://www.bsi.bund.de/dok/TR-03110), particularly for terminal authentication and CVC certificates.
