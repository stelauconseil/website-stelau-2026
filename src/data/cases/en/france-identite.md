## Digital identity requires several kinds of evidence

France Identité brings together mobile application security, electronic identification requirements and European recognition. Stelau supported Agence France Titres across three complementary processes: **CSPN** application certification, certification of the **electronic identification means at the high assurance level**, and the **European peer review**.

The work connects the actual protection mechanisms to assessment requirements, then makes that demonstration clear and verifiable.

## Preparing the application assessment

Our contribution combines **security targets** and **cryptographic specifications** with an analysis of sensitive flows: authentication, user data handling and server communications.

Audits and penetration tests inform hardening recommendations. The areas examined include communication protection, encrypted storage and device security mechanisms. Exchanges with the **CESTI** security evaluation centre support the formal assessment process.

## Documenting high assurance

- Mapping critical functions and eIDAS requirements.
- Demonstrating authentication mechanisms and their reliability.
- Preparing technical and organisational documentation.
- Supporting the ISMS and its alignment with ISO/IEC 27001.

## Preparing the European review

The peer review subjects the French implementation to a shared examination with other Member States. We help prepare assessment checkpoints, formalise evidence, analyse interoperability and support exchanges with the review working groups.

## Results and ongoing work

The iOS and Android applications obtained **CSPN certification** and **elementary qualification**. The electronic identification means was certified at the **high assurance level**, with a positive European review.

Ongoing work addresses maintaining security, extending features and preparing for evolving European digital identity journeys. Certification is a milestone; teams must then maintain their evidence and adapt protections as the system evolves.
