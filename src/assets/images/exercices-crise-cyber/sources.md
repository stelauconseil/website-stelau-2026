## Prompt du visuel de débriefing

Mode : génération complète, outil intégré imagegen, fond opaque, sans image cible. Les photographies Stelau ont été consultées uniquement pour définir l’ambiance, sans être transmises comme cibles d’édition.

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: new landscape editorial photograph for Stelau's cybersecurity crisis-exercise page, section 'Lessons last beyond the exercise'. Generate an entirely new scene with fictional people, not an edit or reconstruction of existing people. Scene: a candid post-exercise debrief in a bright contemporary French office/workshop, pale oak table, warm wooden chairs, neutral walls, daylight through tall windows, a few indoor plants, understated colorful notes. Small group of FIVE consulting engineers and organisational colleagues, mixed genders, ages roughly 25 to 50, everyday professional casual shirts and knitwear, no suits-and-ties staging. They sit in a loose semicircle around the table in a genuine conversation: one consultant listens carefully and takes handwritten notes, another points to a paper sequence of event cards, two participants compare observations and exchange a subtle relieved smile after a demanding simulation, a fifth thoughtfully contributes. Show learning and attentive reflection, not an emergency or a victory pose. On the table: notebooks, ordinary laptop with unidentifiable softly blurred content, printed event sheets and modest colored sticky notes arranged in chronological order; a few follow-up notes set aside. These simple artefacts suggest cross-checking the exercise chronology and agreeing improvement actions. Background whiteboard with a restrained row of colored event cards, NO readable written text. Composition: documentary handheld-camera feel, at participants' eye level or slightly above, medium-wide shot, keep all five heads and expressive hands visible, coherent anatomy, quiet natural energy, authentic varied faces with skin texture, comfortable imperfect real-work atmosphere. Visual style: match natural Stelau team photographs: soft bright daylight, pale wood, warm neutral skin tones, muted clothing with restrained blue/orange accents, modest depth of field, photographic realism and normal office clutter. Avoid overpolished stock-photo aesthetics, posed smiles to camera, blue cyber lighting, neon, hoodies, padlocks, screens full of code, giant floating icons, legible logos, company badges, watermarks or text. Never portray these fictional people as existing Stelau staff. Landscape approximately 3:2, suitable for a 594px-wide / 400px-high website image with important subjects safely within the central frame.
